# Cover letter for Conservation Genetics Resources

Fei Xia · Department of Integrated Bioscience, Graduate School of Frontier Sciences, The University of Tokyo, Kashiwa 277-8561, Chiba, Japan · xf@g.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp

Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript “MiCucumber — in silico design and evaluation of mitochondrial primers for sea cucumber eDNA metabarcoding” as a Methods and Resource Article in *Conservation Genetics Resources*.

MiCucumber provides 29 primer pairs from three mitochondrial loci and three supplementary oligonucleotides for sea cucumber surveys. The manuscript examines three assays in detail using reference-based estimates of target matching, non-target matches and taxonomic resolution. The complete catalogue, comparator and evaluation records give researchers a common basis for testing assays across loci and product lengths.

This study used public sequence data and computational evaluation. The results identify trade-offs and poorly represented lineages that can guide validation with tissue DNA, mock communities and environmental samples. The resource is relevant to monitoring harvested sea cucumber assemblages, including groups containing taxa listed in Appendix II of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora.

The manuscript discloses generative AI assistance with analysis scripting and drafting.
