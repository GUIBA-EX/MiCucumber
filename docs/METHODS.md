# MiCucumber

Following Occam’s razor, retain one D portfolio per length: SD with modest degeneracy (2/8), MD under the strict background gate (64/4), LD for its coverage gain (32/1), and unchanged XLD (2/1). More aggressive SD, MD 128/4 and marginal-gain XLD variants remain comparisons. Ordinary, A, B and S-original are unchanged; all four variants in each length share identical F/R start/end coordinates. This is an explicit benefit–complexity judgment, not a preregistered threshold or experimentally established PCR optimum.

The non-Apodida common panel weights labels equally and haplotypes equally within labels. Coverage allows ≤2 mismatches per primer with IUPAC-compatible terminal three bases; exact matching requires zero mismatches at both ends. Every concrete variant has no terminal homotriplet, at least one GC in the last three bases, 1–4 GC in the last five, and no homopolymer longer than four.

Primer3: 50 mM monovalent salt, 1.5 mM Mg, 0.6 mM dNTP; 50 nM per variant and 200 nM per direction. Hairpin/dimer Tm <55°C; F/R Tm-range midpoint difference ≤5°C. Background gates: SD ≤50, LD ≤20, others ≤5 accessions under both WC≤3 and GT≤2. S-original is an unchanged control. WC≤4 is a sensitivity analysis, not a selection gate.

Existing shared loci remain fixed. Expansion uses ≥1% reference support and multiple degeneracy caps, followed by the retention decisions above. At 200 nM per direction, each MD forward variant is 3.125 nM. LD hits 1,168 background records under WC≤4, a substantial specificity tradeoff. Design and evaluation share references; wet-lab validation remains outstanding.
