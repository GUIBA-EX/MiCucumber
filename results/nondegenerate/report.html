四个区域各1对、2对，共8套无简并候选；另保留v4原版。所有序列均为5′→3′，两对按独立PCR评估。
Eight A/C/G/T-only portfolios plus the unchanged original v4 control. Two pairs mean separate PCRs, not a validated multiplex.

计算候选，尚无湿实验验证 / Computational candidates; no wet-lab validation.

结果判断 / Interpretation: 无简并化显著降低覆盖与完全匹配；此版保留为实验对照，不替代当前简并版。原版v4覆盖较高，但非目标命中很多。 / Removing degeneracy substantially reduces coverage and exact matching. Retain these as experimental comparators, not replacements for the degenerate portfolios. Original v4 has higher target coverage but extensive background hits.

方法与边界 / Methods and limits

枚举当前四区域精简方案中8个简并配对的所有具体序列组合；不进行任意正反引物跨亲本重组或新位置搜索。剔除50/200 nM下预测结构Tm≥55°C的组合。对每区域穷举1对及2对方案，先最大化共同面板与本区域面板的非Apodida平均覆盖，再在两面板各自容差内最大化完全匹配率。v4opt/M两对容差2个百分点，其余1个百分点。最优性仅限这一候选池和目标函数；并未证明全局最优。非目标由全序列双链扫描复核，WC≤3和GT≤2各≤5条背景记录。

Enumerate concrete variants of the eight frozen parent pairs, with no cross-parent F/R recombination or new binding sites. Exhaustively compare one-/two-pair subsets after a structural Tm <55°C filter at 50 and 200 nM per oligo. Optimize mean non-Apodida coverage on common and own-region panels, then exact matching within 1 pp of each panel's coverage anchor (2 pp for v4opt/M two-pair sets). Conditional optimum only. Independently recheck whole-sequence target and background hits.

覆盖：每条引物≤2处错配且3′末端3 nt完全匹配；完全匹配：正反均0错配。共同面板151个名称标签、351个标签内单倍型，非Apodida为145个标签；按标签及标签内单倍型等权。名称未经同物异名统一。背景5,600条，命中为accession数而非reads比例。WC≤4为更宽松的残余风险检查；GT为允许G–T配对的敏感性模型。v4原版不受新候选非目标门槛约束。设计与评估共用参考库，不能当作独立外部验证。

Coverage permits ≤2 mismatches per primer with the terminal 3 nt exact; exact requires zero mismatches in both primers. Common panel: 151 taxonomic name labels / 351 within-label haplotypes; 145 non-Apodida labels. Equal label and within-label haplotype weights; synonyms not reconciled. Background counts refer to 5,600 reference accessions, not read fractions. WC≤4 exposes relaxed residual risk; GT permits G–T pairing. Original v4 is an unconstrained historical control. The reference set is shared between design and evaluation.
