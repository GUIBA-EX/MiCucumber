方法边界 / Scope

覆盖要求每条引物≤2处错配、3′末端3个碱基完全匹配；完全匹配要求正反引物均0错配。Apodida不参与优化目标但仍计入全部目标指标。当前名称改变不意味着序列或结果改变。WC≤4显示更宽松条件下的残余风险；未采用独立留出参考集。/ Coverage: ≤2 mismatches per primer and exact terminal 3 bases; exact: zero mismatches in both primers. Apodida is excluded from optimization but included in all-target reporting. Renaming changes neither sequences nor metrics. WC≤4 measures relaxed residual risk; no independent holdout set.
